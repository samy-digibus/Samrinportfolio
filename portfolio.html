Who I Am
Bridging the Gap Between
Raw Data & Real Strategy

My professional journey started as a Mathematics Teacher, where I first learned the art of breaking down complex concepts. Moving into my B.Sc. in Computer Science (where I spearheaded complex Exploratory Data Analysis), I quickly realized that while I could write code, my true superpower was understanding the data itself. Instead of getting lost in syntax, I thrived on interpreting datasets, uncovering trends, and translating analytics into strategic business decisions.

This analytical mindset naturally led me to roles where technology meets consumer behavior. After a foundational Quality Assurance Internship at Creative Soft Technology, I relocated to Malaysia.

In Malaysia, I joined Tin City Impact as an SEO & Web Intern where I mastered the intersection of technical SEO, content strategy, and website auditing. I diagnosed complex search bottlenecks and orchestrated comprehensive digital campaigns for global brands, ensuring every technical fix directly contributed to increased domain trust and user engagement.

I later brought this focus on the customer lifecycle to Australia. Working with Yes Optus, I refined my skills in high-stakes CRM systems (Jarvis), sales analytics, and regulatory compliance. My greatest strength is my ability to bridge rigorous technical operations with empathetic, cross-cultural communication—consistently turning everyday interactions into measurable business success and exceeding stringent KPIs.
